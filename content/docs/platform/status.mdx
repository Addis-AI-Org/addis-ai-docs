## Live status

Incident history and uptime history are available on [status.addisassistant.com](https://status.addisassistant.com).

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Operational | The service is available and performing normally. |
| Degraded Performance | The service is available, but latency or error rates are elevated. |
| Partial Outage | A subset of users, regions, or features is unavailable. |
| Major Outage | The service is unavailable or severely impaired for most users. |
| Maintenance | Planned maintenance is in progress. |

## Support escalation

If all systems are operational and your request still fails, contact `support@addisassistant.com` with the endpoint, status code, request ID, and approximate request time.

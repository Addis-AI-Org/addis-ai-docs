title: Deprecations and Voice 1 migration
description: Migrate legacy audio integrations to Addis Voice 2.

<StatusBadge status="deprecated">Legacy API</StatusBadge>


The legacy `/api/v1/audio` endpoint and Voice 1 names are deprecated. New integrations must use Addis Voice 2 through `addis.voice.generate` and discover IDs with `addis.voices.list`.

Migration steps:

1. Replace the legacy audio route with the Voice 2 SDK resource.
2. Map a catalog voice returned by `voices.list`; `am-hamen` is the canonical example.
3. Replace character-based cost assumptions with **5 ETB per generated minute**.
4. Choose `mp3_44100`, `wav_44100`, or `pcm_16000`.
5. Add an idempotency value and test clip history/download behavior.

Historical character-priced clip records remain readable. Do not reinterpret them as minute-priced usage.

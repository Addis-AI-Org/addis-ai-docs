---
title: Limits
description: Design for request, upload, and rate limits.
---


Limits can vary by capability and account. The SDK guidance for speech-to-text is a maximum file size of 25 MB and maximum duration of 120 seconds. Validate both before upload.

Applications should also cap prompt length, attachment count, conversation history, concurrent requests, and function-calling rounds. Treat `429` as backpressure: honor `Retry-After` when present and retry with exponential backoff plus jitter.

Voice catalog size is dynamic; query `voices.list` rather than embedding a count. Contact support through the developer portal for account-specific capacity.
